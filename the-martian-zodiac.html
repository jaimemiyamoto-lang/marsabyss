🪐 Understanding the Martian Year

A Martian year lasts 687 sols (Martian days). One sol is about 24 hours 39 minutes — slightly longer than an Earth day. The calendar is divided into 12 signs, each spanning 57 sols (approximately 58.6 Earth days).

The Martian year begins on the Spring Equinox in the Northern Hemisphere. For easy reference, the table below shows the approximate Earth date ranges for each sign.
