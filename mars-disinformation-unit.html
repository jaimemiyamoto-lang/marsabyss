PUBLIC STATEMENT

On the Protection of Martian Citizens From Coordinated Information Threats

The Mars Abyss Disinformation Unit is the public-facing defense of Martian sovereignty in the information domain. We exist to ensure that every citizen of Mars has access to verified information, and to counter the coordinated campaigns — foreign and domestic — that seek to undermine the colony's stability through deception.

Our work is conducted at the intersection of intelligence analysis, strategic communication, and cyber defense. We monitor information channels, verify sources, and publish factual counter-narratives when false ones are identified. We do not silence speech. We do not police opinion. We counter coordination — the organized, industrial-scale production of lies.

This broadcast is updated continuously. Citizens are encouraged to verify information through official channels and to report suspicious activity to their local dome authority. A well-informed colony is a resilient one.
