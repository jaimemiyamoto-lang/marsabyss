Why This
World Matters

"What parts of humanity do we take with us to the stars?"

MY PURPOSE

"The Mars Abyss is my personal exploration of humanity's future. It is a future where alliances between Puerto Rico, Spain, and China have forged a path of unity, proving that compassion can overcome corporate greed. But it is also a world where other nations fractured into isolation. I explore the essential question: What parts of humanity do we take with us to the stars? And what do we leave behind?"

"The very language of Mars is a testament to this unity. It is a creole forged from the Ainu, Mayan, and dozens of Earth dialects spoken by the migrant workers who built our domes. When you see the signs of our cities, you are reading the poetry of human cooperation."

"Earth's history was dominated by a patriarchal hierarchy that prized dominance over sustainability. When we rebuilt society on Mars, we corrected that imbalance. Today, the Martian Regency and the scientific councils are equally represented by women and men, because a society that values life cannot afford to silence half of its population."

WHAT MAKES OUR WORLD SPECIAL

Hope & Survival

From the Martian Survival Manual to Dome Cities, we explore humanity's capacity to adapt and thrive in challenging environments.

Compassion Across Worlds

The Martian Children's Sanctuary represents our highest ideal: that no child should suffer, whether born of Earth or Mars.

Cultural Bridges

Through the Earth Codex and the Borikén Alliance, we honor our roots while reaching for the stars. We show that unity is possible.

The Human Spirit

Martial arts, poetry, and fashion—the beautiful expressions that make us uniquely human, preserved for future generations.

Wonder & Exploration

From solar system exploration to alien encounters, we celebrate the endless curiosity that drives humanity forward.

The Threat of Greed

Not all of Earth chose unity. We explore the dangers of corporate greed and isolationism that threaten to destroy our homeworld.

VOICES OF EARTH

Soren Lindqvist

The legendary Nordic exobiologist spent a century showing us the fragile beauty of our planet. His work reminds us that we are a part of nature, not apart from it.

In The Mars Abyss, his documentaries are preserved in the Atlantis Library, required viewing for every Martian colonist before they are allowed to step foot on the red planet.

Environmental Books & Nobel Laureates

From Lars Magnus's "The Silent Earth" to the climate research of the Tharsis Laureates, these books are the blueprint for survival.

We feature a curated library of environmental texts, including works on renewable energy, ecological preservation, and sustainable agriculture. Because you cannot build a future on Mars if you forget how to save the Earth.

THE CREATOR'S JOURNEY

This universe wasn't born in a boardroom. It was born in a humble house in the Puerto Rican countryside with curtains for doors and mosquito netting around the bed. It was born from a childhood staring at the stars through the cracks in the wooden walls.

After years of writing, a magazine sent a letter that could have ended the dream. Instead, it became the fuel to keep going for another 64 years.

"Although we cannot publish your three stories at this time, we were impressed by your imagination and strongly encourage you to continue writing. The world needs more storytellers with your vision. And we certainly got a kick out of the last one."

— JR Adams / Tetrón
