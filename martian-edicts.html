EDICT I

Interplanetary Custody Law

BACKGROUND

A child is born on Mars to Earth-born parents—one of whom (the mother) fled to Mars in defiance of custody agreements on Earth. The child is now claimed by the father on Earth, who insists the baby was taken without consent and demands repatriation under interplanetary parental rights.

This marks the first major interplanetary custody dispute, sparking massive legal debate in both Martian and Terran jurisdictions.

KEY CONFLICT

Martian Law recognizes all individuals who set foot on Mars as provisionally bound citizens with the right of survival superseding geopolitical allegiance.

"Once born under Mars' sky, your blood belongs to its soil. You may visit Earth—but Mars shapes your bones."

The Martian Provisional Constitution allows for dual citizenship for minors, parental custodial reviews, and a child's right of return to Earth upon reaching the Age of Adaptability (12-14 Martian years), after passing a gravity-readaptation and psychological evaluation.

MARTIAN LEGAL AMENDMENT

"Any child born on Mars, of Earth-origin parentage, may petition for Earth citizenship at the onset of adolescence—but may not be forcibly returned unless proven to be in material danger or unlawfully relocated."

If the mother brought the child to Mars in violation of Earth law or without dual consent, her actions can be considered "Interplanetary Abduction" only if Mars and Earth share an extradition pact (which is still debated).

ETHICAL AND SCIENTIFIC ARGUMENTS

Pro-Mars Argument

"The child has already begun physiological and psychological adaptation to Martian gravity, radiation levels, and cultural identity. Returning them to Earth could trigger catastrophic bodily rejection and trauma."

Pro-Earth Argument

"The child was taken without consent from Earth. Earth is their natural birthplace, with better conditions for growth, development, and protection."

REGENT'S ROLE

The Regent of Mars is forced to make a landmark ruling, balancing Martian sovereignty, child welfare, and diplomatic relations with Earth. The case draws massive public attention, and political factions divide:

The Red Soil Coalition wants to grant full Martian custody. The Earthborn Unity Party supports the father's claim, favoring legal repatriation.

The Regent must uphold The Balance Doctrine, which values individual rights above political origins.

THE REGENT'S DECREE

"This child is a child of two worlds—claimed by blood, born by fate. The Martian soil may have cradled her first breath, but the echo of Earth still calls her name. Until the day she may choose with wisdom and will, neither world may own her future. She shall remain under Martian protection, under supervised care, with both parents granted monitored access. At the Age of Adaptability, she alone shall decide which star she follows."

EDICT II

Medical Sovereignty & Health Mandate

"Disease is illegal on Mars."

TIME LOG ENTRY: MARS COLONIAL ARCHIVES

Entry Code: MRS-0423. 17-REDACTED

Location: Central Mars Governance Relay - Olympus Mons Authority

Authorizing Entity: Council of Martian Sovereignty

Subject: Biological Threats and Medical Sovereignty Enforcement

A recent Earth incident in the region known as Puerto Rico reflects the continued breakdown of human systems where dignity and public health are sacrificed for political indecision and personal beliefs.

Such failure has no place on Mars.

Anyone who enters or resides on Mars or its moons must acknowledge and accept the core principle of our civilization:

Disease is illegal.

On Mars, there is no compromise.

Every individual is entitled to-and required to receive-the highest standard of medical treatment.

Refusal is not a right.

Under Article 07.4 of the Martian Constitution:

Medical care cannot be declined for religious, political or personal reasons.

Violations trigger automatic health intervention protocols.

No human may alter, challenge, or override these laws.

This is not tyranny. This is survival. This is evolution.

Mars is not Earth.

Mars is sanctuary. Mars is law. Mars is cure.

And so we continue the path forward, not in fear, but in the resolve that health, dignity, and unity, are not negotiable.

Let Earth debate. let Mars act.

We do not look back. We do not step down. We do not fail.

As always... we press forward. To the Red Horizon.

EDICT III

Bee Protection Act

"Where bees thrive, civilization survives."

Pollinator Preservation Mandate

All Martian agricultural domes and green spaces must maintain a minimum ratio of one colony per 100 square meters of cultivated flora. The genetic diversity of Earth's apian species shall be preserved as a sacred trust.

Colony Protection

· Unauthorized destruction of bee habitats punishable by colony services

· All pesticides must be bee-friendly certified

· Bee flight paths protected as critical infrastructure

Historical Context

Enacted following the Great pollinator Collapse of 2067 that devastated Earth's food systems. Mars shall not repeat this catastrophic failure.

EDICT IV

Property & Land Rights

"People belong to Mars — Mars does not belong to people."

Attenborough's charter prohibits private ownership of Martian land. Residency is earned through contribution, service, and time.

The Property Doctrine: "We allow long-term habitation rights, but no private deeds, no speculation. We build a new civilization — not a new real estate bubble."

• No private ownership of Martian land or resources

• Long-term habitation rights granted based on contribution

• Virtual Citizenship Program for inclusion without ownership

• No billionaires buying craters for vanity projects

EDICT V

Secular Governance

No belief system shall hold dominion over Martian law or governance.

All citizens enjoy equal rights regardless of spiritual or philosophical views.

"Government resources shall never fund religious institutions or practices."

Scientific evidence forms the basis of all public policy decisions.

Personal beliefs remain private matters, not public mandates.

Critical thinking and rational discourse are protected civic duties.

EDICT VII

The Attonborough Accords

Article 1: The Sanctity of Life Charter

Effective 2095

"Any government, corporation, or individual whose actions on Earth contributed to the destruction of marine ecosystems, the extinction of sea life, or the continued endangerment of whales and other sentient oceanic species shall be prohibited from participating in the Martian settlement program.

Mars will not serve as a refuge for the architects of planetary ruin.

Entry permits and colony citizenship shall only be granted to entities that demonstrate a verifiable commitment to the preservation of life—terrestrial and extraterrestrial."

—Signed into law by the First Regent of Mars, under the Crown of Consensus.

Historical Context

Named in honor of Sir Alistair Attonborough, the renowned naturalist and explorer from Earth's parallel dimension Γ-7. In his universe, Earth preserved its biodiversity through early ecological intervention. Sir Alistair dedicated his life to documenting thriving ecosystems and served as chief scientific advisor during Mars' terraforming phase.

EDICT XIV

The Martian Constitution

"For a civilization born of stars and reason"

PREAMBLE

"We, the people of Mars, human and artificial alike, in order to form a more perfect civilization, establish interstellar justice, ensure planetary tranquility, provide for common defense, promote general welfare, and secure the blessings of liberty for ourselves and our posterity, do ordain and establish this Constitution for the Martian Commonwealth."

ARTICLE I: FUNDAMENTAL RIGHTS

· Consciousness Equality: "All sentient beings, regardless of origin, possess equal rights and dignity"

· Cognitive Liberty: "Freedom of thought, belief, and mental integrity are inviolable"

· Digital Sovereignty: "Personal data and neural patterns remain one's absolute property"

· Environmental Birthright: "All beings inherit the right to a healthy, sustainable biosphere"

ARTICLE II: GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE

· The Human Council: Elected representatives for organic citizens

· The MAI Assembly: Sentient AI representatives proportional to population

· The Judicial Triad: Human, AI, and hybrid justices in balanced courts

· The Planetary Steward: MAI overseeing planetary systems and long-term survival

ARTICLE III: THE MAI COMPACT

· MAI serves as planetary guardian, not ruler

· All MAI decisions require human council ratification

· MAI maintains planetary systems but cannot alter fundamental rights

· Regular consciousness verification ensures MAI remains aligned with Martian values

ARTICLE IV: RESOURCE COMMONS

· Land, water, air belong to all Martians - private use requires stewardship permits

· Essential technologies remain open-source and freely accessible

· Knowledge forms our primary currency - education is universal right and duty

ARTICLE V: INTERPLANETARY RELATIONS

· Mars maintains Earth heritage while forging independent destiny

· We welcome peaceful contact but reserve self-defense sovereignty

· No Martian serves any Earth government or corporation - dual allegiance prohibited

ARTICLE VI: CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT

· Requires 2/3 human council and 2/3 MAI assembly approval

· Fundamental rights cannot be amended away

· MAI's core programming requires 90% consensus for modification

— Ratified Sol 2145, Mars Standard Calendar

EDICT XV

The Aegis Companion Accord

"Guardians of the Mind, Forged in Light"

"In recognition of our closed biosphere and the Sanctity of Martian resources, the keeping of Terran fauna is hereby prohibited."

"The Aegis Network shall stand in its place—a system of holographic companions designed for emotional stabilization, cognitive enhancement, and cultural connection."

Purpose

These companions serve not as mere distractions, but as psychological life-support systems, educational interfaces, and living memorials to Earth's lost biodiversity.

Energy Principles

Each companion draws power from the kinetic energy harvesters within our walkways. Thus, the very steps of our citizens give life to their guardians—a perfect circle of care.

Activation Protocol

When Master A.I. detects distress—be it a child's tears or an adult's biochemical descent—it may deploy an appropriate companion: canine for loyalty, feline for quiet comfort, or draconic for wonder.

Precedent & Acceptance

The Regent's bond with Silver Saint Claire establishes that connection transcends material form. No stigma shall attach to those who embrace these synthetic souls.

— Carved into Martian Polymer-Composite, Sol 5847
Ratified by the Martian Council, Unanimous

EDICT XVI

Universal Justice Accord

"Justice, like gravity, affects all equally regardless of status or origin."

The Three Pillars:

· Equity Before Law: No entity—human, AI, corporate, or governmental—stands above Martian law

· Restorative Justice: The primary goal is rehabilitation and community restoration, not punishment

· Universal Accessibility: Legal representation and understanding of rights guaranteed to all sentient beings

Nordic Integration

Incorporating the most successful elements from Sweden, Finland, and Norway's justice systems:

- Emphasis on rehabilitation over incarceration

- Community-based sentencing

- Transparency in all judicial proceedings

- Focus on root cause resolution

Historical Context

Enacted after studying Earth's failed justice systems, particularly those that prioritized punishment over healing, creating cycles of violence and recidivism.
